# Sutr

A notes app for Android that keeps everything on your phone: a journal, tasks
and projects, ideas, quotes and quick notes, all written in one place and found
again by searching. No account, no ads, no tracking.

The name comes from the Sanskrit *sutra*, a thread.

## What it does

- **One place to write.** A journal entry, a task, an idea or a quote starts the
  same way; you never pick a type first.
- **Today**: what is due, what you wrote today, and what you wrote on this day
  in earlier years.
- **Tasks and projects**, with due dates, repeats and reminders.
- **Search** across everything, and saved searches for what you look at often.
- **Backups you own**: a ZIP of plain Markdown files, saved to a folder you
  choose or, if you connect it, to your own Google Drive, encrypted.
- **Imports** from Diarium and Google Keep.

## Privacy

Your entries stay on your phone. The app reaches the internet for two things
only: your own Google Drive, if you connect it for backups, and a daily check
of this repository for a newer version, which sends nothing about you and can
be turned off.

The full policy: [Privacy policy](privacy.md). The terms: [Terms of use](terms.md).

## Reporting a problem

Settings → Diagnostics builds a text report about your phone and the app's
settings and errors. It never contains anything you wrote, and nothing is sent
automatically: you see it, then share it yourself. Send it, with what you were
doing, to [Issues](https://github.com/Rohith-G1024/Sutr-Releases/issues).
